2026-04-27 - Wi-Fi stock timing lead
====================================

Goal
----
Use the working stock-image capture from debug/017 as the new basis for
the ROCKNIX Wi-Fi bring-up.  Stop treating PB1/gpio73 as a Wi-Fi power
regulator unless new evidence appears.

Stock evidence
--------------
Instrumented stock image:

    debug/v1.1.3-stock-debug-4g.img

The device booted, Wi-Fi worked, and stock settings scan showed nearby
networks.  Captured logs:

    /storage/rppocket-stock-debug-1706104711.txt
    /storage/rppocket-stock-debug-1706104736.txt

Key working-stock facts:

    - Realtek USB device appears as 0bda:8179
    - product is "802.11n NIC", manufacturer "Realtek"
    - it is on DWC2: Linux 4.4.189 dwc2_hsotg
    - vendor driver is rockchip_wlan/rtl8188eu/8188eu.ko
    - wlan0 is created after the vendor driver binds
    - gpio115 / PC3 is vcc5v_en_gpio and is out hi
    - gpio73 / PB1 is not a Wi-Fi regulator in stock runtime
    - pin 73 is owned by ff450000.dsi.0 / lcd_rst group

Important timing difference:

    Stock:
        ~  2.69s panel power path runs
        ~ 24.91s DWC2 root hub appears
        ~ 25.47s Realtek 0bda:8179 appears
        ~ 28.10s wlan0 is created

    ROCKNIX:
        DWC2 root hub appears early, around 1-2s, and never sees the
        Realtek child device.

Hypothesis
----------
ROCKNIX is probing DWC2 too early relative to the panel/PC3 5V enable or
some related board power sequence.  A delayed DWC2 unbind/rebind after
the panel is stable may cause the internal RTL8188EUS to enumerate.
