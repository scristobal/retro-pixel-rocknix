Stuff that's broken or unfinished
---------------------------------
[BUG]  Brightness rocker has no visible action.  The latest known
       working image maps the observed PA0/PA1/PB2 contact group to
       BRIGHTNESS-UP / BRIGHTNESS-DOWN / BRIGHTNESS-CLICK, but the
       user confirmed it does not change brightness.  The original
       RPPOCKET userspace implements brightness as Fn + volume, with
       Fn on BTN_TRIGGER_HAPPY4 / gpio2 PA4; PA4 has not fired in our
       captures.  No clear vendor path for a direct brightness rocker
       has been found.

[TODO] Volume centre press is registered as gpio3 PB7 /

Open questions
--------------
1.  Are the rocker switches actually wired to gpio2 PA2 and PA4 on
    this device, or is the BSP's mention of those GPIOs a leftover
    from the RG351MP design that does not apply to RPPocket
    physically?  Latest evidence points to PA2/PA4 being leftovers:
    the original RPPocket DTS identifies volume up/down as gpio3
    PD3/PC4 instead.

2.  Does the brightness rocker actually have three distinct
    switches, and should ROCKNIX bind the observed PA0/PA1/PB2
    contacts to a brightness action directly?  The vendor behavior
    points instead to Fn + volume, but the Fn GPIO from the vendor
    tree has not been observed firing.
